    Terms of Service & Data Privacy Agreement

    Republic of the Philippines · Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

    1. Official Healthcare Purpose

    MediQueue is the official municipal queue management and appointment scheduling portal for the City Health Office. By using this service, you authorize our triage officers and physicians to schedule, manage, and process your consultation requests.

    2. Information We Collect

    In strict compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), we collect only the minimum necessary personal information:
    • Full Legal Name: For patient verification and counter triage.
    • Contact Email: To dispatch your digital queue pass, reference code, and appointment reminders.
    • Requested Clinical Service: To route you to the appropriate medical staff.
    • Approximate Device Location: Only when you choose "Use my location" to identify your nearest health center.

    3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

    Your personal health data is treated with utmost confidentiality under the physician-patient privilege and the Data Privacy Act. We implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. We will never sell, lease, or disclose your information to commercial entities or third parties without your explicit authorization.

    4. Automated Notifications

    By confirming a booking, you consent to receive electronic notifications (appointment pass, queue status updates, and critical community health mission alerts) via email.

    5. Your Rights as a Data Subject

    Under RA 10173, you retain the right to be informed, access, rectify, or request deletion of your appointment records by presenting your reference code at the municipal health station.

    ⚠️ Consent Required: You must accept the Data Privacy Agreement to schedule clinical appointments and receive official queue passes. You may review and accept whenever you are ready.
